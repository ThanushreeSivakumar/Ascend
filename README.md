## 🧭 Overview

Most career growth advice is generic, static, and disconnected from what's actually happening in an industry *right now*. **Ascend** fixes that.

Ascend pairs every user with **Igris** — an intelligent AI mentor — who takes a single, targeted domain (e.g. Machine Learning, Product Design, DevOps, Finance) and builds a **comprehensive, hands-on roadmap to mastery** around it. Instead of a one-size-fits-all course, users get a living, adapting mentorship system that reacts to the market as fast as the market moves.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🎯 Personalized Domain Mastery
Ascend doesn't try to teach everything — it goes deep on **one chosen career field** at a time. Every recommendation, task, and resource is tailored specifically to that domain, not a generic "learn to code" checklist.

### 📅 Daily Actionable Tasks
Every day, Igris delivers **one custom task** designed to build practical, real-world skill. Small, compounding, consistent — growth by repetition, not by binge-watching tutorials.

### 🔍 Curated Opportunities
Ascend automatically surfaces:
- Relevant **hackathons**
- Open **job listings**
- **Networking** avenues

All filtered specifically for the user's chosen focus area, so opportunity-hunting stops being a full-time job of its own.

### 📡 Industry Intelligence
Igris continuously aggregates:
- Trending **LinkedIn discussions**
- Relevant **news articles**
- **Technology updates**

...so users stay ahead of market shifts instead of reacting to them a year too late.
